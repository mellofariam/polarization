# FigS1 Contact Probability Captions

## fig-chr-contact-probability-normalized-lamina-effect-homolog-averaged.pdf

Homolog-averaged chromosome contact probabilities for chromosomes contacting or not contacting the nuclear lamina. Inter-chromosomal contacts were identified in each simulation frame from Voronoi-neighbor bead pairs separated by less than 1 um; chromosomes with at least one bead on the convex-hull lamina surface were classified as lamina-associated. Contact matrices were normalized by the number of frames in which each source chromosome was in or out of the lamina, homolog self-pairs and zero entries were omitted, and the four maternal/paternal homolog combinations for each chromosome pair were averaged before plotting.

## fig-chr-contact-probability-normalized-nucleoli-effect-homolog-averaged.pdf

Homolog-averaged relative chromosome contact probabilities for chromosomes associated with or excluded from nucleoli. Inter-chromosomal contacts were identified from Voronoi-neighbor bead pairs separated by less than 1 um; for each frame, chromosomes were classified as nucleolus-associated when their minimum distance to a nucleolus was <= 0.10 um and as outside nucleoli when the distance was > 0.10 um. Contact counts were normalized by the number of valid in- or out-of-nucleoli frames for each source chromosome, zero entries and homolog self-pairs were omitted, the four maternal/paternal homolog combinations were averaged, and each state-specific matrix was divided by the summed in- and out-of-nucleoli signal for the plotted chromosome pair.
